The One-Page Resume Press

Paste what you have and the posting you want. This page reorganizes your material into a one-page, ATS-friendly layout, shows which of the posting's keywords you already have and which you're missing, and lets you edit the sheet directly. Everything runs in your browser — nothing is sent anywhere.

The ground rule: this tool only reorganizes what you paste — it never invents jobs, dates, numbers, or skills. Keywords the posting wants that your resume doesn't show appear as an amber list beside the page. Add them to the sheet only if they're genuinely true.
