About

Your resume, tailored to a defense industry job.

DIB Jobs tailors your resume to a job posting, free, for sales, business development and capture people in the Defense Industrial Base. It also gives recruiters a candidate screen.

I have been a seller, a partner, a manager and a leader above managers.

What DIB Jobs does

It tailors your resume to a specific posting, for people in defense industry sales, business development and capture.

  • For job seekers. Add your resume and a posting. You get your resume rewritten for that job as a Word document, with each reworded line shown next to the one it came from. Where the posting asks for something your resume leaves out, it asks you, and adds only what you confirm. You also get the requirements recruiters screen on first set next to what your resume shows, and what the job should pay.
  • For recruiters. Add a job and a stack of resumes. You confirm the must-haves, then get a ranked shortlist with the same checks for every candidate and a note for the hiring manager.

Why it is free

There are no accounts, no ads and no sign-up. If that ever changes, this page will say so first.

What it is not

  • The scores and calls are AI estimates. They are a second opinion, not a hiring decision and not career advice.
  • It does not apply for you. It is told to use only what is in your resume and what you tell it, and it highlights any line it cannot trace back to either. Check every line of a tailored resume before you send it.
  • It is not affiliated with the U.S. government, Amazon, or any employer listed on the site.
