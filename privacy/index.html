Privacy

What happens to your resume

Plain answers about what leaves your browser, where it goes, and what is kept.

Last updated November 2026

The short version

No accounts, no ads, no sign-up. Your resume is read in your browser. When you press a button that runs a check, its text and the job description go through the DIB Jobs server to Google's Gemini AI, and the server does not keep them. The site counts page visits and button presses, never what you typed.

What is sent for analysis

  • Resume and job text. Sent when you run a check, tailor a resume, write outreach, or ask the AI a question. The DIB Jobs server passes it to Google's Gemini API and returns the answer. It does not log or store resumes, job descriptions, questions or answers. Google's Gemini API terms apply to that step.
  • Job links. When you give a link instead of the posting text, the link (not your resume) is sent to Jina AI's reader service, which fetches the page so you can check it before anything is scored.
  • Resume files. PDF and Word files are read in your browser and only their text is sent. If a PDF cannot be read in the browser, the file itself is sent once to extract its text, and is not kept.
  • Recruiters. The same applies to candidates' resumes. With "Hide names" on, the name, email and profile links the page can find are removed from the text before it is sent.

Do not submit classified, controlled, export-controlled or other restricted information.

What stays in your browser

  • Your saved resumes, your job tracker and your settings are kept in your browser's local storage on your device. They are not sent to a server and I cannot see them.
  • On the recruiter page, the job, candidates and ranking are kept in the browser tab only and are gone when you close it.
  • The Word download of a tailored resume is built in your browser.
  • To remove everything, clear site data for dibjobs.com in your browser.

What DIB Jobs counts

The site uses Google Analytics 4 to count visits: pages viewed, browser and device type, approximate location and the site you came from. It sets cookies to tell visits apart.

It also records named events, such as a check being completed, a tailored resume being downloaded, a link being shared, or a thumbs up or down on a result. A completed check records the fit score and whether the military option was on. A thumbs up or down records your vote, the score, and the reason you picked if you picked one. No event carries your resume, the job description, names, or anything you typed.

You can stop this by blocking analytics cookies or using Google's opt-out browser add-on.

What the server keeps

The server keeps short technical logs: the kind of request, how long it took, and whether it failed. It uses your network address for a few minutes to limit how fast any one visitor can send requests. It keeps no record of the text you sent.

What DIB Jobs does not do

  • No accounts and no email list.
  • No advertising, and no sale or rental of data.
  • No use of your resume or a candidate's resume for anything except answering your request.

Links to other sites

Job searches and employer links open LinkedIn, ClearanceJobs, Indeed, Google and company career sites. Those sites have their own privacy policies.
